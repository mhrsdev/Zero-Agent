# Contributor License Agreement (CLA) — Process

**Status: process defined; canonical agreement text pending owner publication.**

Zero accepts contributions under the Apache License 2.0. To keep the project's
licensing clean and to give the maintainer the ability to relicense or enforce
the project's rights in the future, every contributor must sign the project's
Contributor License Agreement before their pull request can be merged.

## Current process

1. Open your pull request as usual (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
2. A maintainer will provide you with the current canonical CLA document to
   review and sign.
3. Once your signed CLA is recorded by the maintainer, your contribution can be
   merged.

## Important limitation

The authoritative CLA legal text is owned and published by the project owner
(`mhrsdev`). This file intentionally does **not** reproduce or invent that legal
text. Until the owner publishes the canonical document in this repository (for
example as `CLA.txt`), the process above applies and merging remains gated on
the maintainer-recorded signature.

No contribution will be rejected because of this gate once the signed CLA is on
file; the gate exists solely to protect contributors and the project alike.
